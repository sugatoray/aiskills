# Examples: `/commentdrop`

List of example prompts and their outputs for the usage of `/commentdrop` skill alongside `/artwallbg` skill.

<!--- example:start --->
## Example-01

1️⃣ Prepare a image first.

<!--- prompt-block:start --->
```prompt
## ---
## metadata:
##   date: "2026-11-08"
##   model: "gpt-6.1-terra"
##   effort: "medium"
##   app: "chatgpt::chat"
## ---

/artwallbg [[
a girl in front of a huge wall painted with spray paint
]]
```
