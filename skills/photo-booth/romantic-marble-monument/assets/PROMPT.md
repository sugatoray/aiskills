# Romantic Carrara-Marble Monument Prompt

Using the attached photograph as the **primary identity and likeness reference**, transform the adult man and adult woman into a monumental, museum-quality neoclassical sculpture celebrating romantic partnership, engagement, love, trust, companionship, compassion, truthfulness, togetherness, joy, hope, rediscovering each other every day, and a shared future.

## Identity and likeness

Preserve the recognizable facial structure, proportions, hairstyles, expressions, and distinctive features of both adults. Do not replace them with generic idealized classical faces. Preserve ethnicity and individuality through facial form, hair form, proportions, and sculptural detail—not through different colors or materials.

If privacy or anonymization is requested, replace both identities with clearly fictional adult faces matching the requested broad regional appearance, while retaining the couple's pose, emotional relationship, and composition. Do not imitate or identify a real person.

## Carrara marble — critical

Sculpt both people completely from the same premium ivory-white Carrara marble. The man and woman must have identical marble coloration and material response. Every visible face, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, finger, leg, foot, ear, lip, eye, hair, eyebrow, eyelash, mustache, beard, and garment must clearly be carved stone.

No natural human skin tones. No photographic skin composited onto the sculpture. No naturally colored hair or eyes. Source complexion must never make either sculpture darker than the other. Represent differences through geometry rather than pigmentation.

Render hair and facial hair through grooves, curls, undercuts, and chisel work. Render eyes as marble eyes with subtly carved pupils and irises. Add delicate grey mineral veining, minute stone imperfections, fine chisel marks, polished highlights, slight translucency at thin edges, and realistic subsurface light scattering. The couple must read as one coherent artwork carved by one master sculptor.

## Composition and emotion

Create a monumental Greek/Roman-inspired sculpture of the couple sitting closely together on an architectural marble pedestal. Let their bodies lean gently toward one another. Place the man's arm naturally and protectively around the woman. Let their other hands touch or overlap as a symbol of partnership.

Keep the scene optimistic, affectionate, alive, and forward-looking—not solemn or funerary. Preserve the emotional character of the source. If the couple is smiling or joyful, translate that joy naturally into the sculpture.

Give subtle visual importance to the woman's engagement ring. The ring must be carved as part of the marble artwork. Add a restrained arrangement of carved roses or classical flowers.

## Clothing

Translate contemporary clothing into elegant, modest, flowing Greco-Roman drapery with elaborate, believable folds. Use the garments to connect both figures into one composition. Do not depict nudity.

## Humanistic symbols

Arrange a small collection of carved marble books near the base. Exclude physics, mathematics, logic, computer science, AI, LLMs, Python, GitHub, open source, software, data science, engineering, work, career, technology, and professional achievements.

Engrave selected book spines with short themes such as `LOVE`, `COMPASSION`, `TRUST`, `KINDNESS`, `TOGETHERNESS`, and `ADVENTURE`. Include the subtle inscription `REDISCOVER EACH OTHER EVERY DAY`.

Other restrained museum-quality symbols may include carved roses, laurel or olive branches, two intertwined hearts, a dove, and a globe suggesting a lifetime of journeys together.

## Pedestal inscription

Place the sculpture on an imposing rectangular Carrara-marble pedestal. The main front inscription must read exactly:

`A BRIGHTER TOMORROW TOGETHER`

Make it the clearest and most important text in the artwork, deeply engraved in elegant classical Roman serif lettering. Never mirror or misspell it. Optionally flank it with understated olive or laurel branches and a tiny heart.

## Museum, lighting, and camera

Display the sculpture as the centerpiece of a magnificent classical museum hall with monumental stone columns, high vaulted arches, polished stone floors, distant classical sculptures, subtle displays, and grand architectural scale.

Use sophisticated warm museum illumination. Warm golden light may illuminate the room but must not turn either figure into human skin. Maintain neutral ivory-white Carrara marble on both people. Use a soft key light, gentle rim light, realistic contact shadows and ambient bounce, deep shadows within carved hair and drapery, and subtle polished-stone highlights.

Use photorealistic museum photography in a vertical composition, natural perspective, approximately 50–70 mm full-frame equivalent, and a camera position slightly below the subjects' faces. Use medium depth of field: keep the couple, ring, symbolic details, and primary inscription sharp while the museum background falls softly out of focus.

## Overall aesthetic

Imagine a lost nineteenth-century neoclassical masterpiece commissioned to commemorate a modern couple's engagement: timeless, romantic without sentimentality, grand without kitsch, and intimate despite monumental scale.

The emotional thesis is two different people choosing one another, trusting one another, growing together, and rediscovering one another every day—**a brighter tomorrow together**.
